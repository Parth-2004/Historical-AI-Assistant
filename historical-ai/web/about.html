A historical AI with a deliberately narrow horizon.

Historically Bounded AI is an offline-first retrieval-augmented assistant whose usable knowledge ends on 31 December 1899.

It combines local semantic retrieval, temporal guardrails, grounded generation and output validation. The goal is a dependable interface to a defined historical archive, not a general-purpose chatbot.

Refusals and missing evidence are treated as product behavior rather than hidden failures.
